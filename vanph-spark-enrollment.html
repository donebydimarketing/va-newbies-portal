Why Spark

Spark is where trained VAs go from finished with Starter to placed with a real foreign client. It's open year-round, so there's no single reopening date to wait for.

Members are grouped into rolling pairing batches, so the sooner you enroll and finish your requirements, the sooner you're placed with a host client.

Is Spark Right for You

Spark is for members ready to work with a real foreign client. Read this fully before you enroll. There are no refunds under any circumstance, for any reason.

You Belong in Spark If

  • —You have a computer and stable internet
  • —You can commit at least 2 hours every training day
  • —You can commit up to 4 hours max per day once paired with a client
  • —You finished Starter or already have an equivalent foundation
  • —You are ready to be evaluated by a real client, not just a coach
  • —You will follow VANPH policies and protocols without needing reminders
  • —You are 18 or older

You Do NOT Belong If

  • —You don't have reliable access to a computer or internet
  • —You can't realistically give at least 2 hours daily during training
  • —You are looking for guaranteed income, not real experience
  • —You expect a refund if it doesn't work out for you
  • —You skipped the fundamentals and are not ready for a live client

Quick Answers

What happens after I submit payment?
Screenshot your GCash confirmation and send it to our Facebook page via DM, along with the details on the next page. Once confirmed, an admin will tag you and let you into the VIP Facebook group.
What if slots fill before I finish paying?
Enrollment closes the moment the batch is full. There is no guaranteed hold once you leave this page without paying.
When do I get paired with a host client?
Once you're placed in a batch, you'll have 2 to 3 weeks to finish your requirements. If you finish on time, you're paired with a host client in that batch's pairing round. If you don't finish in time, you move to the next pairing batch, which opens 3 weeks after the first. Members still not ready by then move to the third batch.
