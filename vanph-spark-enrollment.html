Quick Answers

Do I need to finish Starter first?
No. Starter is not a requirement, and all the Starter materials are included in Spark anyway. Come in as a beginner if that is where you are.
Do I need experience?
No. Most people who join have never worked with a client. That is the whole point of Spark.
What if I am bad at it at first?
You will be, and so was everyone. Better your first rejections happen here, with Coach Di watching, than with a paying client who simply stops replying.
What happens after I submit payment?
Screenshot your GCash confirmation and send it to our Facebook page via DM, along with the details on the next page. Once confirmed, an admin will tag you and let you into the VIP Facebook group.
When do I get paired with a host client?
You will have 2 to 3 weeks to finish your requirements. If you finish on time, you are paired with a host client in the first pairing batch. If you do not finish in time, you move to the next pairing batch, which opens 3 weeks after the first. Members still not ready by then move to the third batch.

It runs for the 5th year celebration. When it ends, Spark goes back to ₱2,500.
